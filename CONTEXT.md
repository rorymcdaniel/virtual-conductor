# Virtual Conductor

Virtual Conductor supports playing a solo part with accompaniment.

## Language

**Wait point**:
A performer-defined measure and beat at which accompaniment must wait for the
soloist before continuing. A wait point is a performance instruction, independent
of musical notation such as a fermata.

**Expected entrance**:
The solo entrance the accompaniment is waiting to recognize at a wait point
before continuing.

**Tempo tolerance**:
The allowed percentage deviation of accompaniment tempo from the planned tempo
at the current musical location while following the soloist.

**Free following**:
Tracking the soloist's position through the written solo part during a performer-configured
unaccompanied passage, allowing extreme rubato and pauses without the global
tempo tolerance.
